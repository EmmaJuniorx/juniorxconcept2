08 — FAQ

Got questions?
Let's clear them up.

What services does Juniorx Concept offer? +

We offer graphic design, branding, web development, custom digital solutions, motion graphics, media content and printing services.

Do you design and print? +

Yes. We can handle the creative design and printing for applicable projects, helping keep your brand consistent from screen to physical material.

Can you build a custom website? +

Yes. We work on business websites, landing pages, portfolios and more customized web applications and digital platforms.

How much does a project cost? +

Pricing depends on the requirements, complexity, quantity and specifications of the project. Contact us with your idea for a quote.

I'm not sure what I need. Can you help? +

Absolutely. You don't need to have everything figured out. Tell us what you're trying to achieve and we'll help you work out the direction.
